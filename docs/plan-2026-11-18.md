## 7. Card payment by phone (Sola PhonePay)

The caller types the card on the keypad on Sola's own secure line. We never hear, record or keep card numbers.

**Flow**

1. Box 140: the card line plays; 0 = a payment link instead; no key = straight on.
2. The hotline dials Sola PhonePay ((833) 434-3354, backup (844) 422-7356) and sends the PIN, the exact order amount (from the order, never the caller) and 1. With payment music on, a separate call does this while the caller hears the music.
3. The caller types the card; star brings them back at any time.
4. Our server checks Sola's record every second while the call to Sola is up.
5. Back on our line, the try is matched in Sola's record by amount, time and the caller's phone number in the Invoice field. We never guess: if two charges could be this caller's, box 147 plays.
6. Paid: box 148. Not paid: try again, up to the limit.

**Approved lines**

- Box 147: "Sorry, we could not confirm your payment. To try again, press 1."
- Box 148 (plays ONLY after Sola's record shows the payment approved): "You are all set, {name}! Your payment was approved, and your booking is confirmed. Your order number is {number}. We will text your confirmation to the number you are calling from. To send it to a different number, press 1. Thank you for choosing Hup Up Attractions!"
- The old "You are all set" line before payment is removed; a test keeps it that way.

**Limits (Admin settings).** 3 card tries per call. 5 calls with card tries per caller number in 24 hours; past that, card by phone is blocked for that number, you are emailed, and staff unlock it on Admin > Card blocks. A blocked number gets the payment link instead. Wrong amounts are voided and you are emailed. The third-decline and blocked-number lines: waiting for your wording.

**Hanging up Sola: option a (your choice, Nov 18)**

- Twilio sends the audio of the call to Sola to a small helper on your free Cloudflare account (Media Streams, about 1 cent per payment call). No Google listening. Nothing is recorded or kept; the helper only measures when Sola is talking.
- Never hang up while the customer is paying. Only after Sola's record shows approved or declined, wait for Sola to finish its sentence (about half a second of quiet), then hang up; the caller comes back to our line.
- The 4-minute timer is removed completely.
- Silence check: 20 seconds (Admin setting) with no Sola voice, no key tones and no result: check Sola's record once more. A result is used. Still nothing: hang up Sola and play box 147.
- If the helper fails: option c, checking Sola every second and hanging up a set number of seconds after a decline (Admin setting, 6 seconds); an approval is never cut off.
- Status: built and on Cloudflare (hupup-sola-listen), commit dbc1a82. Switched OFF until a real test call. Then I send you the results, and you tick "Sola hang-up helper" on Admin > Phone > Sola PhonePay.
